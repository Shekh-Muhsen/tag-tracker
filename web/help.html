Google Find Hub

Shows only the last location. No history — you can't see where your tag has been.

Tag Tracker

Saves every location over time: full route, day→year history, replay, and theft alerts.

↻ How a location reaches your phone

  • 1
    Your tag broadcasts a tiny encrypted Bluetooth signal every 1–2 seconds.
  • 2
    Any Android phone passing nearby (~10–30 m) detects it and uploads the encrypted position to Google — silently and anonymously.
  • 3
    Your phone asks Google for it over the internet, and only your phone can decrypt it (that's the Unlock step).
  • 4
    It's drawn on your map, saved to history, and synced to Google Drive.

Updates come whenever a phone passes the tag — often in busy areas, rarely in empty ones. Your phone receives over the internet, so the tag can be anywhere.

⚑ First-time setup

  1. Sign in to GoogleUse the account your tag is on. This lists your tags.
  2. Unlock encryption requiredEnter your phone's screen lock on Google's page — this lets your phone decrypt locations. Without it the map stays empty.
  3. Turn on background checkingThe app checks every 15 min and saves each location, using almost no battery.
  4. Sync to Google Drive recommendedDefault mode auto-saves to your Drive, so a new phone gets your history back automatically.
  5. Park & guardTap Park when you leave the bike — you'll get a loud alert if it moves.
